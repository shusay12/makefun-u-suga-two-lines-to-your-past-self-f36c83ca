## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, no network) writes exactly two
lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

> When the doctor says "come back in six months," don't nod and walk out — book the scan at the desk before you leave the room.
> That scan would have caught it while it was still small enough to cut out, and the next two years would have been yours, not the hospital's.

Line 1 warns my past self about one specific moment — the doctor's
"come back in six months" and the walk out of the room. Line 2 traces the
cause and effect: the missed scan, and the two years that followed belonging
to the hospital instead of to me.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (266 chars, 2 lines)
```

`run1/entry.txt` and `run2/entry.txt` are the outputs of two runs of the
final program, byte-identical.
